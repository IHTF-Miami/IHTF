    El BULULÚ. ANTOLOGÍA ENDIABLADA

    MDCA’s On.Stage Black Box Theatre | 2901 West Flagler Street, Miami

    (Free parking)

    TEATRO AVANTE | Miami, Florida, USA

    Bayamesa

    by Abel González Melo | Directed by Mario Ernesto Sánchez  (World Premiere)

    Carnival Studio Theater, Adrienne Arsht Center | 1300 Biscayne Boulevard, Miami

    ​

    Co-presented by the Adrienne Arsht Center for the Performing Arts of Miami-Dade County

    Co-sponsored by MIAMI-DADE COUNTY DEPARTMENT OF CULTURAL AFFAIRS , NATIONAL

    ENDOWMENT FOR THE ARTS, FLORIDA DEPARTMENT OF STATE DIVISION OF CULTURAL AFFAIRS, FUNDING ARTS NETWORK (FAN)

    ​

     

    A theatre company comes across unpublished manuscripts of the poet María Luisa Milanés (1893-1919), an ardent feminist and patriot born in Bayamo, Cuba, and undertakes to put on a play based on her complicated life, thereby discovering the fascinating story of a dangerous clash with patriarchal tyranny.

    ​
