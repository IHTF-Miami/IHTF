TEATRO LA MORENA | Montevideo, Uruguay

NO DARÉ HIJOS, DARÉ VERSOS (I Won’t Bring Forth Children But Poems)

CACERÍA (The Hunt)

LOS ESPEJOS DE DON QUIJOTE (Mirrors of Don Quixote)

Written and directed by Alberto Herreros Salcedo
A fictional prison encounter between Miguel de Cervantes and William Shakespeare where they exchange experiences, passions, and reflections on madness, genius, and the immortality of their creations.

LA ISLA DESIERTA (The Desert Island)

Written by Roberto Arlt and José Menchaca | Directed by José Menchaca
In a drab port office, clerks trapped in routine and monotony dream of escaping to paradisiacal islands. Performed in complete darkness using auditory, tactile, and olfactory stimuli.

TEATRO AVANTE | Miami, Florida, USA (World Premiere)

EL PUERTO DE LOS CRISTALES ROTOS (Harbor of Broken Glass)

Written by Mario Ernesto Sánchez and Patricia Suárez | Directed by Mario Ernesto Sánchez
The tragedy of the Jewish refugees aboard the MS St. Louis who sailed from Germany in 1939 seeking asylum in Havana and Miami, only to be denied entry and forced back to the Nazi horrors. A historical tragedy confronting the present on intolerance and human refuge. (In Spanish with English supertitles)

Co-sponsored by Miami-Dade County Department of Cultural Affairs, National Endowment for the Arts, and State of Florida Division of Cultural Affairs.

EL SISTEMA SOLAR | Lima, Peru

EL SISTEMA SOLAR (The Solar System)

Written and directed by Mariana de Althaus
During a tense Christmas Eve dinner in Lima, a dysfunctional family confronts buried secrets, reproaches, and unhealed wounds when the elderly father introduces his new partner—his own son's ex-girlfriend. A poignant yet tender portrait of family dynamics. (In Spanish)

Sponsored by Miami-Dade County Auditorium | Co-sponsored by Costamar Travel.
