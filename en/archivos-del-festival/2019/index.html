NUEVE DE NUEVE | Zaragoza, Spain

LA EXTINTA POÉTICA (Extinct Poetics)

Written by Eusebio Calonge | Directed by Paco de la Zaranda
What does Ophelia do in our era? Who hears her lament before she sinks into vulgarity and tedium? It is like asking for the meaning of poetry in our age of speed and space.

Co-presented by Adrienne Arsht Center | Sponsored by the Government of Aragón, Spain | Co-sponsored by Centro Cultural Español Miami, Cultural Office of the Embassy of Spain, Spain Arts and Culture, City of Zaragoza and City of Huesca.

COMPAÑÍA TEATRO IMAGEN | Santiago, Chile

CARTAS DE JENNY - La intranscendente epopeya de Jenny Masterson

(Letters from Jenny - The Insignificant Epic of Jenny Masterson) | Written and directed by Gustavo Meza
A domineering mother, an overprotected son and a girl who comes to break up the circle of maternal certainties with her charm and cheekiness.

Sponsored by FONDART Nacional 2019, Ministry of Cultures, Arts and Heritage of Chile | Co-sponsored by Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Consulate General of Chile in Miami.

COMPAÑÍA DE TEATRO FUGAZ | Buenos Aires, Argentina

EL BULULÚ. ANTOLOGÍA ENDIABLADA (The Itinerant Actor. A Devilish Anthology)

Written & Directed by Leticia González de Lellis and Osqui Guzmán
Theatre is a sewing machine that stitches memories together. The writings from Spain’s Golden Age become fused with the gold that the conquistadors took from Bolivia. It is cultural gold that moves across time and that only memory saves by honoring the troubadour's art.

ALBANTA | Cádiz, Spain

BENDITA GLORIA (Blessed Gloria)

Written by Juan G. Larrondo | Directed by Pepe Bable
Following her father’s death, Gloria shuts herself up in her home like a hermit inside a labyrinthine retreat, becoming usurper, mistress and slave in a prison that houses other ghosts with no less tangled histories.

Co-presented by Adrienne Arsht Center.
Carnival Studio Theater, Adrienne Arsht Center | 1300 Biscayne Boulevard, Miami

LOS CLAVELES | Murcia, Spain

SIETE CABRITILLOS (Seven Little Goats)

Written by Paca García and Aniceto Roca | Directed by Aniceto Roca
Disobeying their mother, seven kids (little goats) let a wolf into their house. But their mother, and the courage and ingenuity of the youngest, deal the fierce intruder his just deserts. (In Spanish)

TEATRO AVANTE | Miami, Florida, USA

BAYAMESA

By Abel González Melo | Directed by Mario Ernesto Sánchez (World Premiere)
A theatre company comes across unpublished manuscripts of the poet María Luisa Milanés (1893-1919), an ardent feminist and patriot born in Bayamo, Cuba, and undertakes to put on a play based on her complicated life, thereby discovering the fascinating story of a dangerous clash with patriarchal tyranny. (In Spanish with English supertitles)

Co-presented by Adrienne Arsht Center | Co-sponsored by Miami-Dade County Department of Cultural Affairs, National Endowment for the Arts, Florida Division of Cultural Affairs, Funding Arts Network (FAN).
Carnival Studio Theater, Adrienne Arsht Center | 1300 Biscayne Boulevard, Miami
World Premiere

Sunday, July 28 | 5:00 pm
EL PRINCIPITO PRODUCCIONES | Buenos Aires, Argentina

MILLONES DE SEGUNDOS (Millions of Seconds)

Written & Directed by Diego Casado Rubio
Since he was 5, a transexual teen has been counting the seconds he has left to live in an alien body. He is 554 seconds old and still harbors the hope of beginning his transformation. (In Spanish with English supertitles)

Co-sponsored by Fondo Nacional de las Artes Argentina, Pro Teatro, Ministry of Culture of Buenos Aires, FIBA, Teatro del Abasto.
MDCA’s On.Stage Black Box Theatre | 2901 West Flagler Street, Miami
