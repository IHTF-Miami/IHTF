WATER PEOPLE THEATER | Venezuela / Chicago - USA

LAS DELICADAS LÁGRIMAS DE LA LUNA MENGUANTE (The Delicate Tears of the Waning Moon)

Written by Rebeca Alemán | Directed by Iraida Tapias
Cast: Rebeca Alemán - Gabriel Porras | Voices: Laura Crotte, Anabel Sofía Ybarra, and Miguel Núñez
Inspired by true events, The Delicate Tears of the Waning Moon explores the ongoing human rights violations faced by journalists for speaking the truth. Paulina, a committed journalist and advocate for victims of femicide and indigenous communities, awakens from a four-month coma following a brutal attack. To achieve justice, she must reclaim her memory and reveal the truth.
Westchester Cultural Arts Center | 7930 S.W. 40th Street (Tropical Park), Miami
In Spanish with English supertitles

COMPAÑÍA TEATRO FUTURO | Buenos Aires, Argentina

VIENTO BLANCO (White Wind)

Written by Santiago Loza | Directed by Valeria Lois & Juanse Rausch
Cast: Mariano Saborido
Mario runs an inn with his mother in a remote southern outpost. Beyond it lies a forgotten port that long ago ceased operating. Transient sailors, travelers, passing souls. Once, Mario had a friend. There is a return, a parting farewell, and Mario's yearning to escape forever. Amidst the icy sea, fierce passions, sacred chants, and howling wind. Viento Blanco is an evocative journey where clerical eroticism, Chinese merchants, arctic marine life, humor, and tender melancholy converge.
Koubek Center Theatre | 2705 SW 3rd Street, Miami
Dramatic monologue

COMPAÑÍA CRIOLLA | Buenos Aires, Argentina

EL BROTE (The Outbreak)

Written & Directed by Emiliano Dionisi
Cast: Roberto Peloni
For an actor, the boundaries between fiction and reality begin to dissolve, and he now mistrusts whoever is scripting the events of his life. What kind of character are we in this story? Premiered in February 2023 at Teatro del Pueblo in Buenos Aires, this acclaimed production by Compañía Criolla achieved widespread critical and public acclaim, winning major national and international awards.
Carnival Studio Theater, Adrienne Arsht Center | 1300 Biscayne Boulevard, Miami
In Spanish with English supertitles
Friday & Saturday, September 12 & 13 | 8:30 pm

LOS TRISTES TIGRES | Mexico City, Mexico

LOS QUE SOBRAN (Those Left Over)

Written & Directed by Adrián Vázquez
Cast: Fátima Favela - Quetzalli Cortes - Lariza Juárez - Paula Zepeda - Mónica Vega - Iván Carbajal
Alejandro is unusual; suffering from emotional instability, he prefers to avoid trouble. Sofía, his sister, leads the pack. Edith is a fierce fighter who defends her friends. Emilio is pure chaos. And Camila is the intellectual mascot. Los que sobran tells the story of these five youths who forged an unbreakable bond in adolescence, tempered into lifelong companions by time and adversity. Told through a singular perspective, it is a visceral story of friendship, youth, defiance, passion, violence, survival, and love.
Westchester Cultural Arts Center | 7930 S.W. 40th Street (Tropical Park), Miami
Youth drama

LA TIRANA PRODUCCIONES | Cádiz, Spain

PALABODA (The Wedding)

Written by La Tirana, Rocío Sepúlveda & Juan Sebastián Domínguez | Directed by La Tirana
Cast: Susana Rosado - Jay García
A hilarious comedy recounting the heartwarming story of Begoñita and Juanmanué, who must come to terms over the coveted wedding she eagerly anticipates and he desperately avoids. She wants a grand traditional ceremony; he hates tight shoes; she wears a veil to look slender; he rejects rings because his fingers swell; she dreams of a banquet with cake, dancing, and champagne; he just wants it over quickly so he can go fishing... PALABODA is pure delight and guaranteed laughs.
Koubek Center Theatre | 2705 SW 3rd Street, Miami
Comedy

TRYO TEATRO BANDA | Santiago, Chile

MAGALLANES (Magellan)

Written by Francisco Sánchez & Tryo Teatro Banda | Directed by Francisco Sánchez & Eduardo Irrazábal
Cast: Alfredo Becerra - Javier Bolívar - Diego Chamorro - Martín Feuerhake - Daniella Rivera - Francisco Sánchez
Portuguese navigator Fernão de Magalhães leads a Spanish fleet seeking to reach the Spice Islands of Asia-Pacific by sailing west, searching for an elusive route through the Americas to a new ocean: what would become known as the Strait of Magellan. Mistrust, treachery, perils, mythical beasts, starvation, shipwreck, mutiny, and rebellion threaten the voyage. Evoking medieval jugglers with violin, tiple, accordion, electric bass, and percussion, Tryo Teatro Banda narrates this breathtaking epic, capturing the first encounter between European explorers and the indigenous peoples of southern Chile.
Carnival Studio Theater, Adrienne Arsht Center | 1300 Biscayne Boulevard, Miami
In Spanish with English supertitles

Sunday, September 21 | 5:00 pm

INTERNATIONAL CHILDREN'S DAY | COMICÓPICOS

TEATRO AVANTE | Miami, U.S.A.

LEAR / UMPA! The Living Voice

Inspired by William Shakespeare's “King Lear” | Written & Directed by Neher Jacqueline Briceño
LEAR Cast: Julio Rodríguez - Claudia Valdés - Claudia Tomás - Laura Alemán - Daniel Romero
The crumbling of patriarchal power through a fractured journey across King Lear's memory underpins this contemporary take on Shakespeare's tragedy, where father-daughter confrontations and the price of power take center stage. Among them, the Fool becomes the essential bridge uttering truths that can no longer remain hidden.

UMPA! The Living Voice | Tribute to Mario Ernesto Sánchez
Cast: Alina Interián, Marilyn Romero, Gerardo Riverón, Yani Martín
A scenic celebration featuring Teatro Avante actors honoring the artistic vision and leadership of Mario Ernesto Sánchez, founder of the company and creator of the International Hispanic Theatre Festival of Miami. Two major cultural projects that crossed borders, driven by his tireless passion to champion the performing arts for our community. Today his voice is not history—it is momentum, rhythm, and enduring presence!
Carnival Studio Theater, Adrienne Arsht Center | 1300 Biscayne Boulevard, Miami
In Spanish with English supertitles
