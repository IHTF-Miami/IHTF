LOS TRISTES TIGRES | Mexico City, Mexico

ALGO DE UN TAL SHAKESPEARE (Something by One Shakespeare)

Written and directed by Adrián Vázquez
An invitation to play on the stage, to be irreverent. It is an entertainment composed of improvisations on William Shakespeare’s writings, a playful journey through some of the works of the greatest dramatist of all time. It seeks to bring children and young people closer to Elizabethan theater without boredom. (In Spanish)

Co-sponsored by FONCA México.

FERNANDO RUBIO | Buenos Aires, Argentina

TODO LO QUE ESTÁ A MI LADO (Everything That’s By My Side)

Written and directed by Fernando Rubio
An intimate and immersive theatrical experience. In individual beds set up in the lobby, an actress and a single spectator share a whispered childhood memory, exploring intimacy, subtle movement, memory's drift, and human connections. (In Spanish)

Co-sponsored by Miami-Dade County Auditorium.

AL FIN Y AL CABO | Marbella, Spain

PIEDRAS PRECIOSAS (Precious Stones)

By Pablo Díaz | Directed by Andrea Chacón Álvarez & Raul Mancilla
In the 1960s, artist Jean Cocteau spent several years in Marbella. Piedras preciosas imagines the relationship between Cocteau and Manuel, a young flamenco dancer, showing their mutual learning and affection. (In Spanish)

Co-sponsored by Otoclinic, Teloneras, Ayto. Marbella, CCEMiami, and Miami-Dade County Auditorium.

MÁRMORE - COMPANHIA DO CHAPITÔ | Lisbon, Portugal

ÉDIPO (Oedipus)

Collective creation from the classic Oedipus of Sophocles | Directed by José Carlos García
Oedipus sets out to escape his inexorable fate of parricide and incest, duel with monsters, and endure tragedies. Companhia do Chapitô recounts this classic Greek tragedy through breathtaking physical comedy in a totally hilarious, inventive way. (In Spanish with English supertitles)

COMPAÑÍA CRIOLLA | Buenos Aires, Argentina

LOS MONSTRUOS (The Monsters)

Written and directed by Emiliano Dionisi
Claudio and Sandra know their children have unique qualities distinguishing them from the rest. Sure they have behavioral issues and are seldom invited to parties, but only a good parent can protect their offspring from a monstrous world. An internationally acclaimed musical drama. (In Spanish)

NIDOS (Nests)

TEATRO AVANTE | Miami, Florida, USA (U.S. Premiere)

NOTAS QUE SABEN A OLVIDO (Forgetting)

By Araceli Mariel Arreche | Directed by Mario Ernesto Sánchez
Making use of theater's power to communicate and acknowledging the need to know more about this cognitive disorder, the play explores the complex situation of those living with Alzheimer's, reflecting on affection, memory, and everyday relationships. (In Spanish with English supertitles)

TRES VIEJOS MARES (Three Old Seas)

By Arístides Vargas | Directed by Arístides Vargas and Charo Francés
Three old men meet by the sea to talk about the twilight of their lives, filled with rivalry, jealousy, disappointment, and illusion against the ocean tides. A moving play on memory, humor, and tender reconciliation. (In Spanish)
