TEATRO AVANTE | Miami, USA

CAROUSEL

Written by Abel González Melo | Directed by Jackie Briceño
Cast: Julio Rodríguez, Alina Interian, Monica Quintero, Isabella Chirinos
In a mathematics class, a teenage girl violently attacks her classmates. Her grandfather, a teacher, and a doctor analyze the minor's strange behavior, opening the door to a past that reveals secret bonds among the four of them that will inevitably dictate their destinies in a recently democratic country that has apparently lived through an extended period of dictatorship.
Westchester Cultural Arts Center | 7930 S.W. 40th Street (Tropical Park), Miami
In Spanish with English supertitles
Wednesday Sept 10, Thursday Sept 11, Friday Sept 12 | 8:30 P.M.

COMPAÑÍA ABUELA KATIUSKA, SALA VERDI & PAZO DA CULTURA DE NARÓN | Montevideo, URUGUAY / Galicia, SPAIN

ZOMBIE MANIFESTO

Written and directed by Santiago Sanguinetti
Cast: Paula Lieberman, Rogelio Gracia, Carmen Laguzzi, Santiago Sanguinetti
The corpse of a military officer vanishes following a clandestine exhumation and abduction. An exemplary bureaucrat demands a dignified burial. The deceased appears and demands justice. Meanwhile, two young people wonder what the best tool for changing the world is: political correctness or class consciousness? Zombie Manifesto is structured in eleven scenes that serve as a reminder that it is not enough to interpret the world in various ways; the point is to transform it.
Westchester Cultural Arts Center | 7930 S.W. 40th Street (Tropical Park), Miami
In Spanish with English supertitles
Thursday Sept 11, Friday Sept 12 | 8:30 P.M.

TEATRO A MIL FOUNDATION | Santiago, CHILE

STORY OF A BOAR (OR SOMETHING ABOUT RICHARD III)

Written by Gabriel Calderón | Directed by Cristian Plana
Cast: Marcelo Alonso & Francisco Reyes
Two actors face the challenge of playing Richard, the ruthless monarch of William Shakespeare's tragedy. They have spent their entire lives in supporting roles and believe they deserve this opportunity. During character construction, affinities between the actors and the English monarch begin to surface. A piece delivering a vital space to reflect on the limits of human ambition, contemporary mechanisms of power, desire, and resentment.
Carnival Studio Theater, Adrienne Arsht Center | 1300 Biscayne Boulevard, Miami
In Spanish with English supertitles
Thursday Sept 11, Friday Sept 12 | 8:30 P.M.
Saturday Sept 13 | 5:00 P.M.
Friday Sept 12: Post-performance discussion
MOLA PRODUCCIONS | Catalonia, SPAIN

ON FIRE (A FUEGO)

Written by Pablo Macho Otero | Directed by Emma Arquillué & Pablo Macho Otero
Cast: Pablo Macho Otero
Erostratus, a shepherd from Ancient Greece, set fire to the Temple of Artemis at Ephesus to go down in posterity. In this monologue entirely in verse, full of satire, wordplay, and metatheatre, the protagonist's fascination with Erostratus becomes an obsession. Jumping from character to character, the performer laughs at his own megalomania, inviting us to reflect on contemporary narcissism and the human desire to leave a mark.
Koubek Center Theatre | 2705 S.W. 3rd Street, Miami
Verse monologue in Spanish

PITCHBLACK | New York City, USA / Buenos Aires, ARGENTINA

ODD MAN OUT

Created and directed by Carlos Armesto, Martín Bondone & Facundo Bogarín
Cast: Agustina Cedraschi, Lorenza Bernasconi, Giorgia Valenti, Mauricio Marte, Facundo Bogarín, Gonzalo Trigueros, D Henry Hanson
ODD MAN OUT is an immersive sensory experience in complete darkness that awakens your senses and imagination. The audience boards a PITCHBLACK Airlines flight and experiences the journey of a blind musician from Buenos Aires through 360° binaural sound, smell, taste, and touch. A transformative theatrical journey named Best Unique Theatrical Experience of 2025 by the Off Broadway Alliance.
Westchester Cultural Arts Center | 7930 S.W. 40th Street (Tropical Park), Miami
Immersive sensory experience in total darkness

COMPAÑÍA CRIOLLA & BUENOS AIRES FESTIVALS | Buenos Aires, ARGENTINA

DREAM (SUEÑO)

Written and directed by Emiliano Dionisi (inspired by Shakespeare)
Cast: Emiliano Dionisi, Lucía Baya Casal, Tomás Castiglione, Marina Caracciolo
A theater troupe gathers in the woods to rehearse a play, unaware that magical creatures are ready to play whimsically with their emotions. Inspired by Shakespeare's A Midsummer Night's Dream, four performers embody all the characters in a theatrical whirlwind of rhythm, physical deployment, poetry, and humor. Winner of the Almagro Baroque Competition and nominated for the Max Awards 2026.
Koubek Center Theatre | 2705 S.W. 3rd Street, Miami
In Spanish

Buenos Aires, ARGENTINA

PUNDONOR

Written by Andrea Garrote | Directed by Rafael Spregelburd & Andrea Garrote
Cast: Andrea Garrote
Claudia Pérez Espinosa, university professor and Doctor of Sociology, returns to the classroom after taking leave. Her introductory lecture on Michel Foucault is repeatedly interrupted by her own fragile emotional state: she feels an urgent need to explain herself and seek redemption before her students. Acclaimed solo performance honored by GETEA and nominated for the María Guerrero Awards.
Carnival Studio Theater, Adrienne Arsht Center | 1300 Biscayne Boulevard, Miami
In Spanish with English supertitles

TEATRO SALVAJE | Miami, USA

INTERNATIONAL CHILDREN'S DAY | THE AMAZING ADVENTURES OF ROBINSON CRUSOE

Written by Victor Hugo Cortés | Directed by Luciano Cortés
Cast: Juan Bautista, Cote Composto
Tells the story of a daring young man who, after being shipwrecked, arrives on a deserted island where he meets unforgettable characters. With creativity and optimism, Robinson finds ingenious ways to survive and learns the true value of friendship. An interactive show connecting audiences of all ages with fantasy, adventure, and the vital power of imagination.
Key Biscayne Community Center & Koubek Center Theatre
Children and family theater | Free Admission

TEATRO LA PLAZA | Lima, PERU

HAMLET

Written by W. Shakespeare | Free adaptation and directed by Chela De Ferrari
Cast: Octavio Bernaza, Jaime Cruz, Lucas Demarchi, Manuel García, Diana Gutiérrez, Cristina León Barandiarán, Ximena Rodríguez, Álvaro Toledo
A group of actors with Down syndrome takes the stage to share their hopes and frustrations through a free adaptation of Hamlet. The production weaves Shakespeare's classic text with the performers' own lives, revolving around existence's fundamental question: To be or not to be? What does it mean to exist for people who fight for recognition? Internationally acclaimed production.
Carnival Studio Theater, Adrienne Arsht Center | 1300 Biscayne Boulevard, Miami
In Spanish with English supertitles
