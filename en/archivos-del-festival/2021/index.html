MURGA MADRE | Montevideo, Uruguay

MURGA MADRE

Written by Pablo Routin | Directed by Fernando Toja | Musical Direction: Edu Lombardo
Silences. Glances that say what is not expressed in words. Two characters lacking names, time and space... a returning through the murga. Something they were... or are. Fragments scattered to complete a story. The eternal flow of life. Yesterday, today and always... a show for the soul.

Musical theatre / Murga

TEATRO DE LA VUELTA | Cuenca, Ecuador

BARRIO CALEIDOSCOPIO (Kaleidoscope Neighborhood)

Written & Directed by Carlos Gallegos
Alfonsito wakes up with a desire to go to the store and buy a loaf of bread. To do so, he must overcome his multiple eccentric fears, appear to be like others and leave his meticulously organized, dusty home. He must walk quickly down the lonely street, avoiding teasing, sadness and any encounter with Machiavellian Magalita.
MDCA’s On.Stage Black Box Theatre | 2901 West Flagler Street, Miami
Dramatic clown

An apparently ordinary man turns his pain into a provocation. With honesty and humor, he shares stories from his life involving a bee sting, a boy and a dog, and a woman.
