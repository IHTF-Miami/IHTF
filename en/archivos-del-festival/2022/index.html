MONTEVIDEO, URUGUAY

BETTE DAVIS, ¿ESTÁS AHÍ? (Are You There, Bette Davis?)

Written and Directed by Domingo Milesi
Azucena is looking for love and the meaning of life following the loss of her mother and her retirement from work. She visits an old neighborhood movie house that refuses to die, where an encounter detonates a game between reality, fiction, and esoteric practices.

The play features binaural sound technology; the audience listens with headphones.
MDCA’s On.Stage Black Box Theatre | 2901 West Flagler Street, Miami
Binaural sound technology
Thursday, Friday and Saturday, July 14, 15 and 16 | 8:30 pm
MURÁTICA TEATRO | Badajoz, Extremadura, Spain

ALACRÁN O LA CEREMONIA (Scorpion or The Ceremony)

Written by José Antonio Lucia | Directed by Román Podolsky
A story of a love triangle in the heart of sordid Spanish taverns. Scorpion or The Ceremony emerges from gypsy splendor amid obscenities, zambra and thorns. Alacrán comes to remember 'La Cangrejo,' the love of his life who is no longer here, settling into the black, funny, and absurd tragicomedy of his life.
MDCA’s On.Stage Black Box Theatre | 2901 West Flagler Street, Miami
Tragicomedy
Friday and Saturday; July 15 and 16 | 8:30 pm
Sunday, July 17 | 5:00 pm
FUNDACIÓN FESTIVAL INTERNACIONAL TEATRO A MIL | Santiago, Chile

ENCUENTROS BREVES CON HOMBRES REPULSIVOS (Brief Encounters with Repulsive Men)

Adapted & Directed by Daniel Veronese
In the course of eight brief independent meetings, two actors interact and exchange roles back and forth, delving into the contemporary masculine condition in love, sex and even loss.
Carnival Studio Theater, Adrienne Arsht Center | 1300 Biscayne Boulevard, Miami
Dramatic comedy

SOCIETY OF THE EDUCATIONAL ARTS (New York) & CONECTA MIAMI ARTS (Miami)

LOS COLORES DE FRIDA (Frida's Colors)

Written & Directed by Manuel Morán
A bilingual performance about the great Mexican painter Frida Kahlo combining music, visual arts, story narratives, puppets and audience participation. Three Fridas on stage transport young audiences on a biographical journey where colors heal.

EL BALCÓN DE MEURSAULT | CABA, Argentina

ROTA (Broken)

Written by Natalia Villamil | Directed by Mariano Stolkiner
A mother must rebuild her existence around the death of her son, who killed himself after committing femicide. The plot centers on the voice of this woman who had to raise her son on her own, searching for understanding.

ENTRETENIMIENTO EDUCATIVO | San Juan, Puerto Rico

QUÍNTUPLES (Quintuplets)

Written by Luis Rafael Sánchez | Directed by Emineh de Lourdes
A national Puerto Rican classic of universal scope that transcends time. Quintuplets is the most viewed theater work around the world written by a Puerto Rican playwright who resides in Puerto Rico.
